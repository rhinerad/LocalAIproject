## What was fixed, so you do not undo it

Four faults, each found by a render failing. All four are already applied to all
124 prompts and to `audio_pass_config_omni_v1.json`:

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| quieter second vocal under every take | `retention_analysis` declaring `<Audio N>: reference` |
| gibberish mid-line | one `<d>` block per scripted line, never split |
| gibberish right after the line | delivery before the tag, mouth closes after it |
| **gibberish filling the tail** | **render short, pad silence at assembly** |

**A fifth was added 2026-08-18 — and it supersedes how the fourth was explained.**

| fault | fix |
|---|---|
| **gibberish or mispronounced words anywhere** | **walk the duration ladder: base, +1, −1, +2, −2, +3, −3 — seed held fixed** |

Words ÷ duration is the speech rate the model has to hit, and only the duration
can move it. A seed re-roll asks for the same impossible rate under different
noise, which is why re-rolling produced a different flavour of gibberish rather
than clean speech. `work/duration_ladder.py` owns the sequence;
`work/render_audio_pass.py` walks it automatically.

**Correction you must not undo.** This document used to say *"H3 fills whatever
window it is given with speech"* and treated that as the cause of fault 4. **It
is not supported.** `4_`.mp4` in the good-audio set is 56% silence and is clean.
The `00B` failures carried a corrupt prepended header *as well as* a long
duration, and only the duration was noticed. Rendering short is still right — it
is ~30% cheaper and all 14 known-good takes are short — but it is a precaution,
not a cure.

```

PICTURE      full    story_config.json            00B  362 frames  15.00 s
ASSEMBLY     pad     +0.63 s digital silence    = 15.00 s
```

### Accepting a take — by ear, audio only

The 0.2 MP picture will look bad. Ignore it completely. Accept when all five
hold:

- the words are the scripted words
- one voice, one dry layer — no quieter duplicate, echo, whisper, or chorus
- **no invented speech after the line ends**
- no second character muttering underneath
- no invented music

**The recogniser cannot decode H3's synthetic voice. An empty transcript means
unknown, never perfect.** Do not mark anything accepted on a transcript alone.

### Re-rolling

Vary the seed first. If a clip fails the same way twice, check its entry in
`audio_pass_durations.json` — a tail of invented speech usually means the render
was still too long for the line. Report it rather than editing the prompt by
hand; the prompts are generated and a manual edit will be overwritten.
