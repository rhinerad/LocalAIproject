# EDITOR OVERRIDE — Post-fall empty handheld POV (Rhine 2026-09-20)

## Standing rule
Once the character picks up the camcorder and falls into the Backrooms, **the character must not appear in any still or video frame**. He is holding the camera for the rest of the film. The viewer only sees through the camcorder. No actor face, body, hands-as-portrait, or third-person view of him in Backrooms shots.

## Transition sequence (rewrite target)
1. Character wants to check what is on the camera.
2. He walks toward the camera.
3. He picks up the camera — the frame starts moving because he is holding it.
4. The room starts shaking; he reacts ("what the—").
5. He falls through the floor with the camera in his hand into the Backrooms and hits the ground.
6. From that landing forward: **empty diegetic handheld POV only** (operator / character completely outside the frame).

## Still / QA implications
- Backrooms stills that show the sitcom actor are automatic REGEN.
- Creative stills QA must treat post-fall Backrooms prompts as `expect_empty_of_people=true` unless an Entity is explicitly in-frame.
- Apartment audition shots (pre-pickup) may still show the character; those will be redone separately.
